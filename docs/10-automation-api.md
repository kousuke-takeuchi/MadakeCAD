# Automation & APIs

[日本語](10-automation-api.ja.md)

Everything the UI can do is scriptable. Three surfaces expose the same Command engine, so automated edits share undo history and update the UI live.

## Available

### MCP server (for AI clients)
Streamable HTTP at `127.0.0.1:9310/mcp` (`MADAKE_MCP_PORT` to change). Tools:
`open_project` / `save_project` (open or save a `.mdkproj`, with its chat history; opening replaces the drawing and is refused while an agent turn is running, and the in-app agent cannot open), `get_project`, `list_symbols` (bundled **and** user symbols), `place_symbol` (returns the new part's pins; attribute `hide_reference: "true"` keeps the designator but does not print it), `draw_wire`, `get_pins` (pins of a placed part: number, name, paper position, direction), `connect_pins` (wire two pins by name, e.g. `U1.SDA` to `J1.2`, with a right-angle route; `connections` wires many in one undoable edit), `place_mate` (a plug/socket pair in one step; `mates` places many; an optional `pole` is stored on both halves and the From-To wire list then writes `REF:POLE` instead of `REF:1`, and a `mates` list that repeats a reference without poles numbers them 1, 2, 3 in list order), `connect_cable` (a multi-core cable drawn as one line on one end or both: `cores` names the cores, `a`/`b` give each end's `pins` or `part` and `style: multi` (one wire per core from the pins to a breakout) or `single` (the line lands on the part); instead of `pins`/`part`, a `multi` end may give `labels` (one net-label name per core, with `breakout_at` required): each core then gets a 10 mm wire running straight away from the run and ending in a net label of that name; breakout wires carry no gauge or colour of their own; cores map to pins in pin order; `cables` draws many in one undoable edit), `execute_commands` (full Command schema exposed), `get_netlist`, `run_verification`, `get_tidy_metrics` (the four counts plus `details`: each counted problem with its kind, entities, text and place, up to 50), `arrange_sheet` (align the parts of a sheet into columns and rows, open routing channels of `channel_mm` (default 8 mm), shrink gaps wider than `max_gap_mm` (default 20 mm), keep the order and redraw the wires of every cluster whose ends moved (clusters whose ends stay keep their wires); ground and power marks and labels that connect to one part only, and labels or marks placed directly on a pin, move with that part; `sheet_id` and optional `options` (`channel_mm`, `max_gap_mm`, `align_mm`, `route`; out of range is refused and nothing changes). A cluster that cannot be routed first keeps its old wire shape, stretched along the moved columns and rows with its ends on the pins (only when every pin, label, branch point and junction of the cluster stays on its wires, and it touches no other line, pin or label and crosses no other part's outline), and is listed in `kept_shape`; otherwise it is joined by net labels named after its own label, a label of the same net on another cluster, its net-name symbol or `ARR_<n>`, and listed in `labelled` (ground and power marks are never deleted). Cables, panel layouts, dimensions, port contacts, wires pointed at by other entities or FreeCAD, mixed length sources, clusters holding labels (or net-name symbols) with different names and results that do not fit the frame or run into the title block (with its revision rows) are refused, naming the nets where nets are the reason. One edit = one undo step. Reply: `moved`, `columns`, `rows`, `routed`, `labelled` (`[{net, label}]`), `kept_shape` (net names), `before`/`after` (the `get_tidy_metrics` numbers), `wire_length_mm` and `size_mm` (before and after)), `reroute_wires` (redraw the wires of a sheet automatically without changing any connection: right-angle wires that avoid symbols, text and other nets with few bends and crossings; `sheet_id`, `nets` (net names; default all) and optional `options` (`grid_mm` 0.5 to 10 mm, costs, clearance and margins finite and not negative; anything else is refused with an error and nothing changes); routing works on each physically connected wire group; groups that a cable run uses, whose wires differ in colour/gauge/part number/wire number or in where their lengths came from (`length_source`), that another entity (cable core, note leader, dimension) or a FreeCAD link points at, whose pin leads would touch another net, or that cannot be routed are left as they are and listed in `unchanged` with the reason; wire attributes carry over and the sum of the old `length_m` values goes on the longest new wire only; the automatic names of unnamed nets (N001 and so on) may change places after a reroute, because the new wires get new ids; one undoable edit, so one undo restores the original wires with their ids; the reply has `rerouted`, `unchanged` (`[{net, reason}]`), `before`/`after` tidy counts and `wire_length_mm` before/after), `simulate_op`, `search_parts`, `upsert_part`, `delete_part`, `import_kicad`, `import_dxf` (DXF from AutoCAD Electrical / EPLAN; optional `wire_layers`), `list_terminal_blocks`, `get_terminal_chart`, `check_terminal_block`, `list_templates`, `apply_template`, `list_macros`, `save_macro`, `apply_macro`, `duplicate_symbol`, `save_user_symbol`, `delete_user_symbol`, the PLC I/O tools, `get_sheet_image` (the sheet as a PNG image, same look as the SVG export; `px_per_mm` and a `region` in mm to zoom in), `export_svg`, `export_pdf`, `export_dxf` (AutoCAD 2000 DXF), `export_kicad` (`.kicad_sch`), `export_report` (one of the seven reports as CSV or framed PDF), `export_pdf_book` (cover + all sheets + selected reports in one PDF), `export_bom`, `export_wire_list`, `undo`, `redo`.
Drawing edits that have no dedicated tool go through `execute_commands`: `set_revisions` (revision table), `set_sheet_annotation_text` (`height_mm` 1.0-3.5mm, or null for the 2.0mm default: the height of the wire notes, wire numbers and net tags on that sheet), `renumber_wires` (`mode: append | renumber`, optional `sheet_id`, `start`) and `set_wire_numbers` for wire numbers, `add_entity` with a `harness` entity for harness boundaries, `update_entity` writing `attrs["jumpers"] = "1-2,3-4"` on a terminal block symbol for saddle jumpers, `set_frame_template` for the frame template, `set_text_font` for the drawing font (`{"text_font": {"id": "kst32b", "latin": "half"}}` letters every text with the bundled stroke font, `latin: "full"` draws Latin letters full width, `null` returns to the system font; an unknown id is refused), `add_sheet` with `kind: "panel"` plus the `panel_outline` / `din_rail` / `duct` / `footprint` / `panel_legend` entities for panel layout, `renumber_panel_items` for balloon numbers, and `set_mech_link` / `remove_mech_link` / `set_wire_lengths` for the FreeCAD link (mechanical links and measured wire lengths).
Port pins: `place_symbol` with `attrs.block_pins` pins that carry `contacts` (`{"no":"U3","name":"USB-A メス","contacts":["VBUS","D-","D+","GND"]}`) makes one pin carry several conductors (2 to 32 contacts per pin, trimmed, unique within the pin); `get_pins` returns `contacts`; `get_netlist` splits such nets into `<name>.<contact>` with `contact` on the net and on port pins; `connect_cable` lands a side on one port pin with `pins: ["39.U3"]` and `style: single` (cores map to same-named contacts, or by order when no name matches and the counts are equal; a partial name match and `style: multi` on a port are refused).
Feed-through pins: one pin number may appear once on each of two opposite sides of a `block_pins` block (left and right, or top and bottom): the same pole with a wire on each side. Both sides need the same name (spaces trimmed) and neither may carry `contacts` or `connector`; the same number twice on one side, on neighbouring sides or three times is refused (`duplicate block pin number`). The two points are one electrical node and the net lists the pin once. `get_pins` returns both; a bare `U1.1` is ambiguous, so name `U1.1#1` or `U1.1#2`: they follow the block's pin table order (left, right, top, bottom as defined, before rotation or mirroring), not the sides as seen on screen, so on a mirrored block `U1.1#1` is drawn on the right. With `block_numbers: "inside"`, the number of a feed-through pair whose two pins lie on one line is drawn once, centred in the box, when the sides crossing that line (top and bottom for a left/right pair, left and right for a top/bottom pair) have no pins; otherwise each pin draws its own number. File format 23.
Connector pictures: a `block_pins` pin may also carry `connector` (one of `usb_a_f usb_a_m usb_c_f usb_c_m micro_b_f micro_b_m jack35_f jack35_m dc_f dc_m screw jst_ph_f jst_xh_f`; `_f` socket, `_m` plug) and, for `screw` / `jst_ph_f` / `jst_xh_f`, `connector_ref` (consecutive pins with the same ref form one body, labelled `J3 PH` / `TB1`). An unknown kind, a blank ref, a ref on another kind and one ref used by two groups are refused. A connector pin's point is 5 mm from the box, so read positions with `get_pins`; `get_pins` and the netlist do not report the kind.
Fine pitch: `place_symbol` with `attrs.block_pitch: "2.5"` (default `"5"`) gives a block a 2.5 mm pin pitch and a 2.5 mm size step, with a 5 x 5 mm minimum; a side with n pins needs at least (n + 1) x 2.5 mm. The outline and the pin rows are shifted by 1.25 mm where needed so that pins and edges stay on the 2.5 mm grid, so read positions with `get_pins`. `attrs.block_numbers: "inside"` (default `"outside"`) draws the pin numbers inside the outline and does not draw the pin names, so show signal names with net labels on the wires. Other values are refused, and a connector picture (`connector`) cannot be used on a 2.5 mm block or on a block with numbers inside.

Drawing appearance: `set_sheet_wire_style` (`sheet_id`, `weight_mm` 0.18-1.0 mm or null for the 0.35 mm default, `hide_notes` true to stop drawing the wire notes — gauge, colour word, length — while the wires keep their colour) through `execute_commands`. All values of this command are always set together: a value that is left out goes back to its default (for example, leaving out `hide_notes` shows the notes again). The junction dot grows with a thick wire (radius 0.9 times the weight, never below 0.6 mm), and the fan-out lines of a cable follow the weight. `place_symbol` with `attrs.block_caption: "inside"` (default `"above"`) writes the reference and the value in the middle of the block outline instead of above it; nothing moves out of its way, so use it on boxes whose middle row is free (a pin name or an inside number on the middle row is overwritten). A note (`text` entity) with `rotation` 90, 180 or 270 is drawn turned clockwise about its anchor point; its selection box and its leader follow the turned text.

Line and lettering style: `set_sheet_wire_style` also takes `symbol_weight_mm` (outline weight of symbols and blocks, 0.18-1.0 mm, null for the 0.3 mm default) `vivid_colors` (true draws wires in pure colours; the colour names do not change) and `hide_numbers` (true stops drawing the wire numbers, that is the net names and numbers written on wires; the names stay in the data); all five values are set together. A `wire` entity may carry `dashed: true` and its own `weight_mm` (0.18-2.0 mm). A `shape` line or polyline may carry `arrow_end: true`. `set_text_font` takes `pen_mm` (0.1-1.0 mm): every text is then stroked at that width whatever its size; `spacing` (0.3-3): a letter-spacing factor for every text of the drawing; and `stretch` (0.3-3): a horizontal scale of the glyphs and their advance, for drawings lettered with narrow characters (it does not apply to frame text, which keeps the widths of its template cells). A `text` entity may carry its own `stretch` too (it multiplies the font's). A `text` entity may carry its own `spacing`, which multiplies the font's (frame template cells multiply it too). `place_symbol` with `attrs.block_leads: "none"` (only with `block_numbers: "inside"`) draws no leads and puts the pins on the block outline, so wires run straight to the box. Any symbol may carry `attrs.caption_layout`, a JSON object `{"ref": {"x","y","h","anchor","hide"}, "value": {...}}` that places the reference and the value line by line (offsets in mm from the placement point, `y` is the baseline, `anchor` start/middle/end, `hide` true to drop the line); a line left out stays where it was.
Claude Code connects automatically via this repository's `.mcp.json`.

### Link API (REST + SSE, for external tools)
`127.0.0.1:9310/api/v1` — plain JSON REST used by the CLI, browser-based UI verification, and (future) the FreeCAD add-on:
- Read: `/project`, `/symbols` (+`/symbols/user`), `/frame-templates`, `/stroke-fonts/{id}` (the glyphs of a bundled stroke font, e.g. `kst32b`; 404 for an unknown id), `/panel/unplaced`, `/netlist`, `/verify`, `/search`, `/devices`, `/parts`, `/wire-parts`, `/terminals`, `/terminals/chart`, `/terminals/check`, `/plc/modules`
- Write: `/commands` (Command array; `?batch=true` runs the whole array as **one** edit = one undo step, and applies nothing at all if a command is refused), `/undo`, `/redo`, `/save`, `/load`, `/import/kicad`, `/import/dxf`, `/simulate/op` (body: `case_id` = a saved study case, `open_switches`, `overrides` = reference → {attribute: value} for one run, `shorts` = net names shorted to ground; the result adds `findings`, rating violations most severe first, and `loads`, current / voltages / limit / limit kind / ratio per wire and part), `/route/reroute` (same body and reply as `reroute_wires`), `/route/arrange` (same body and reply as `arrange_sheet`), `/symbols/user/{save,delete}`, `/symbols/duplicate`, `/macros/{build,save,apply,apply-inline}`, `/templates/apply`, `/variants/{start,finish}`, `/export/{svg,pdf,dxf,kicad,pdf-book,report,bom,wire-list}`, parts CRUD
- Live updates: `GET /events` (SSE patch stream)
- Local-origin guard: requests from non-local web origins are rejected

### madake CLI
Thin terminal client over the Link API: `status`, `project`, `netlist`, `verify`, `sim`, `reroute` (`--sheet <ID>`, repeatable `--net <NAME>`; `--json` prints the raw reply), `arrange` (`--sheet <ID>`, `--channel <MM>`, which also raises `max_gap_mm` to the channel when it is above 20 mm; `--json` prints the raw reply), `parts`, `terminals` (terminal block list), `export`, `save`/`open` (incl. `.kicad_sch` and `.dxf`, the latter with `--wire-layer`), `renumber` (wire numbering: `--sheet`, `--mode append|renumber`, `--start`), `exec` (Command array from JSON), `undo`/`redo`. `--json` for machine-readable output.

`madake export <kind> <path>` covers both schematics and reports:

| Kind | Options |
|---|---|
| `svg`, `pdf`, `dxf`, `kicad` | `--sheet <ID>` (default: first sheet). `dxf` = AutoCAD 2000 DXF for ACADE/EPLAN, `kicad` = `.kicad_sch` |
| `pdf-book` | `--reports wire-list,terminal-chart,terminal-diagram,bom,xref,panel-placement` (in that order), `--no-cover` |
| `bom`, `wire-list`, `terminal-chart`, `terminal-diagram`, `xref-table`, `plc-io`, `panel-placement` | `--format csv\|pdf` (default: from the file extension — `.pdf` writes framed drawing sheets), `--terminal <ref\|id>` for the two terminal reports |

Reports always cover the whole project; the only narrowing is `--terminal TB1` (add `--sheet <ID>` when the same designator exists on several sheets). `madake terminals` lists the candidates.
